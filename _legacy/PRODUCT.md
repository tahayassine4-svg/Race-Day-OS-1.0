# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is Yassine, using the app personally on iPhone to stay consistent with simple daily calisthenics. The secondary user is a discipline-driven calisthenics user who wants a premium, motivating tracker without coaching programs, social features, or account setup.

The user is usually checking the app before or during short workout bursts: at home, between work blocks, or around training sessions. The primary task is to understand today's status quickly, log reps fast, and know whether the day is profitable, on track, or in debt.

## Product Purpose

Getting Money App turns push-ups, pull-ups, squats, and optional dips into a financial discipline loop. Every rep earns money, missed daily reps become debt, and debt can only be paid down by doing more reps. Success means the user opens the app, immediately understands their bank/debt status, logs reps in seconds, and feels pulled toward getting debt back to zero.

## Brand Personality

Premium, disciplined, sharp.

The product should feel like a private banking app for physical discipline: clean, composed, status-aware, and quietly intense. It should feel desirable enough for other people to use while staying personal and focused.

## Anti-references

- Generic fitness app energy: bright neon gradients, cartoon badges, loud confetti, stock workout illustrations.
- Finance parody: fake credit-card clutter, excessive money icons, casino energy, or anything implying real cash value.
- Productivity dashboard clutter: too many cards, charts, and tiny labels fighting for attention.
- Dark-mode-only gym aesthetic: black-on-black, aggressive red, military styling, or overbuilt masculinity.
- Social fitness patterns: feeds, leaderboards, follower counts, public challenges.

## Design Principles

1. **The balance is the interface:** The user should understand bank, debt, and daily status before reading anything else.
2. **Make debt physical:** Debt must feel actionable, not shameful. Every debt state should point toward a clear next rep action.
3. **Fast beats comprehensive:** Rep logging must be faster than opening a notes app.
4. **Premium through restraint:** Use strong hierarchy, native interaction, and exact spacing instead of decoration.
5. **Gamify without noise:** The app can be motivating and playful, but the core tone stays composed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for all text and interactive states. Do not rely on color alone for debt, success, warning, or progress states. Support Dynamic Type where practical, reduce motion for users who request it, and keep all core actions reachable with VoiceOver labels.
